The seventh phase of the Coupled Model Intercomparison Project (CMIP7),
the world's major contemporary project for running and comparing Earth System Models (ESMs),
is underway \citep{dunne_cmip7_2025}.
A key part of model intercomparison is standardisation:
ensuring that assumptions which should be common across models are indeed common
so that the differences which emerge are isolated to factors of interest.
To support this standardisation,
a significant effort is invested in standardisation of model boundary conditions
and the importance of this standardisation is increasingly being recognised \citep{naik_input-data-releases_2025}.
These boundary conditions are referred to as forcings,
because they drive the model's response
(and play a role analogous to the role that force plays in Newton's second law
i.e. if one thinks e.g. in terms of a force and response model).

The process of generating forcings has evolved over multiple phases of CMIP.
There is now an explicit task team dedicated to the production, standardisation and documentation of these forcings datasets,

In this paper,
we document the atmospheric greenhouse gas concentration forcings to be used for the CMIP7 pre-industrial control (piControl) and historical experiments.
We do not document how to use these forcings in the experiment.
Such documentation is best left to the CMIP7 guidance pages, which are kept up to date in real time
(see \newline \mbox{\url{https://wcrp-cmip.github.io/cmip7-guidance/docs/CMIP7/Experiment_set_up_and_Forcings/}})
or papers describing the epxeriments themselves
(which are scientific documents, but cannot be updated as easily in case of small errors being spotted post-publication,
hence our recommendation is still to rely on the guidance pages as they are the most reliable source of up-to-date information).
These forcings will also be used in a number of other experiments that we do not enumerate here.
As above, please see the guidance pages at
\newline \mbox{\url{https://wcrp-cmip.github.io/cmip7-guidance/docs/CMIP7/Experiment_set_up_and_Forcings/}}
or the papers describing the experiments for details about which forcings to use
and how to implement them in your model.
How to record which forcings were used in a simulation
(e.g. how to choose the forcing index, i.e. the `f' in variant labels like `r1i1p1f1')
is not yet settled.
Users should consult the introduction of the guidance pages,
check back for updates frequently and use these pages as the primary source of information.
Following \newline \mbox{\url{https://github.com/PCMDI/input4MIPs_CVs/issues/415}}
may also help track discussions based on a concrete use case.
Recommendations that appear in papers, including this one,
should only be used as a secondary source, because they can quickly become outdated.
